== Description ==

**Present your services professionally with a polished "Our Services" section your clients will trust – built right in the block editor, no code required.**

[**Services Section**](https://bplugins.com/products/services-section/) | [**Demos**](https://bplugins.com/products/services-section/#demos) | [**Pricing**](https://bplugins.com/products/services-section/pricing)

### Why Services Section Block?

**Services Section Block** adds a new block to the Block Editor that lets you create a professional-looking services or "OUR SERVICE" section in minutes. It is very effective at presenting your services nicely, with many options to decorate your section using any colors you like.

Pick an icon from the built-in icon library (or upload your own PNG, JPEG, or JPG), start from a ready-made template, and fine-tune typography for titles and descriptions until the section matches your brand.

### Free Features

- **Icon Library:** Select the icon of your choice from the built-in library, or upload any image in PNG, JPEG, or JPG format as a service icon.
- **Built-in Templates:** Start from ready-made templates whenever you want to present your services.
- **Customizable Elements:** Apply custom typography to the title and description.
- **Color Options:** Decorate your service section using any color you like.

### Pro Version – Unlock Advanced Layouts & Styling

- **Advanced Layout Variations:** Unlock exclusive layouts (Layout 2 to Layout 8) with unique styles and structures.
- **Pro-Level Padding & Spacing Controls:** Set custom padding and spacing per side with more flexibility.
- **Enhanced Border Customization:** Full border controls including individual side styles and advanced radius options.
- **Animation Direction Settings:** Directional animation control for layout-specific designs like Layout 4.

- **Opacity & Hover Opacity:** Control the transparency of the circle background, including on hover.
- **Radius & Border:** Control corner roundness and customize border style, width, and color.

### How to Use Services Section Block – Quick Start

1. Install and activate the **Services Section Block** plugin.
2. In the Gutenberg editor, add the **Services Section** block from the **Widgets** block category.
3. Add your services, choose icons from the library or upload your own images.
4. Pick a template and adjust colors and typography from the right-side settings sidebar.
5. Publish – enjoy!

### Use Cases

- **Agencies & Freelancers:** Present design, development, or marketing services in a clean grid.
- **Local Businesses:** Showcase what you offer with icons and short descriptions.
- **Corporate Sites:** Build a polished "Our Services" page section with consistent branding.
- **Landing Pages:** Highlight key offerings with linked service cards (Pro).

== Installation ==

**From your WordPress dashboard (recommended):**

1. Go to **Plugins → Add New**.
2. Search for **"Services Section"**.
3. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
4. In the Gutenberg editor, search for the **Services Section** block and add it to any post or page.

**Manual installation:**

1. Download the **Services Section** plugin (*.zip file*).
2. In your admin area, go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**, choose the **`services-section.zip`** file, and click **Install Now**.
3. Activate the plugin through the **Plugins** menu in WordPress.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is Services Section Block free? =

Yes, Services Section Block is a free Gutenberg block plugin. A Pro version with advanced layouts and styling controls is also available.

= Does it work with any WordPress theme? =

Yes, it works with any standard WordPress theme.

= Can I use my own images as service icons? =

Yes. If the icon you want is not in the built-in icon library, you can upload any image in PNG, JPEG, or JPG format and set it as a service icon.

= Are there ready-made templates? =

Yes. Several built-in templates are included so you can present your services quickly without designing from scratch.

= Can I add links to service items? =

Yes, in the Pro version you can enable links on items and choose where they apply – on the button, the title, or the entire box – and whether they open in a new tab.

= Can I change block settings? =

Yes, you can change all block settings from the right sidebar of the Gutenberg block editor.

= How many times can I reuse the block? =

You can use the block an unlimited number of times across posts and pages.

= Where can I get support? =

You can post your questions on the [support forum here](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/services-section/)
